# Test and evidence strategy

> Target design / v0.1 / 2026-11-26. Not verified as-built.

- Collect reproducible permitted-source fixtures; test rate limit handling, timeout, retry, change detection and duplicate suppression.
- Validate event/source mapping, market classification, regulatory dates, every figure and regulatory claim with a source link. Sample source agreement and report sample size/error rate.
- Assert weekly digest has item citations and contains only eligible events; stale or unresolved claims flagged.
- Test website/social/video drafts; citation preservation; review/edit/approve state transition; approval invalidation on changes; authorization and no automatic publication.
- Test freshness audit with controlled older and newer source versions.
- Record baseline manual research/writing duration vs assisted duration with comparable tasks and human evaluation. No fabricated participants or benchmark results.
- Business interviews and team feedback are open until genuine evidence exists.
