# Ecomdy Project Constitution

**Version:** 0.1 · **State:** Proposed working agreement; owner ratification pending

## Authority and evidence

1. The official Challenge 2 workbook governs challenge-mandated requirements. The blueprint, business workstream plan, and image notes inform target proposals; they do not override the official brief.
2. Preserve source links, versions, dates, assumptions, conflicts, and verification evidence for material decisions.
3. Distinguish user-approved requirements, official mandatory requirements, mentor recommendations, proposed enhancements, and unverified assumptions.
4. Do not invent sources, interviews, implementation, test results, performance gains, or deployment status.

## Product principles

- Evidence first: every material news item and every figure/regulatory claim must link to source evidence.
- Human editorial authority: generated content remains draft until an authorized person approves the exact version.
- Lawful source access: public access alone does not establish permission; review source terms, limits, and retention before collection.
- Make uncertainty visible: preserve unresolved, stale, contradictory, and inferred states.
- Preserve data lineage and version history; do not silently overwrite source evidence or approvals.
- Minimize data and permissions; isolate secrets, untrusted source text, and provider requests.
- Prefer the simplest architecture that satisfies approved requirements and can be tested.

## Change and review rules

- Every change names requirement IDs, allowed scope, dependencies, acceptance evidence, and rollback or recovery approach where relevant.
- Preserve existing code and history. Do not force-push, bypass branch protection, auto-merge, auto-publish, or deploy without explicit authorization.
- Keep product business logic outside the reusable `.harness/` core.
- Run relevant existing checks; report exact commands, results, and limits. A specification is not an implementation.
- Independent review is evidence-based and may challenge assumptions, scope, security, and accuracy.

## Decisions that remain open

The owner must approve product MVP boundaries, sources, brand inputs, provider/data policy, identity, retention, cost, deployment, and quantitative acceptance criteria. This proposed constitution does not grant authority to publish internal materials or take production actions.
